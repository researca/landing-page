🚀 What are the usecase recommended for Researca?

The most common usecases are:

  • Finding fresh content and generating newsletter issues for your SMBs
  • Staying up-to-date on the latest news for tech workers
  • Show your potential clients that you are an expert in your domain

😥 How do I cancel my subscription?

You cancel it, once you are connected to the app, by clicking on the credit card icon on the bottom left corner.

💳 Is the subscription commitment-free?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription any time, if you cancel it, you go back to the free tier and can keep using the free tier features.

💡 Can I test for free?

Yes currently, the starter fee is not collected, so you can test freely without any credit card. You can even generate 2 newsletter issues for free before subscribing.

⚙️ What is under the hood?

Under the hood, researca is composed of a RSS feed aggregator to collect the news and blog articles. On top of that, we built our own recommendation and scoring engine to ensure you get fast to the most pertinent news. And finally, using latest LLM technologies we make the content generation smooth.

🆘 Can I start on my own?

Yes! Most of our users are starting and generating there newsletters 100% on their own. If you're a big organization feel free to ask for some support to get the machine running.

📰 I want to add a website but I can't find its rss feed.

Sometimes the rss feed is not easy to find but here are some tips:

  • - If it's a wordpress site, you can add /feed at the end of the URL
  • - If it's a substack newsletter, you can add /feed at the end of the URL
  • - For most press websites you will find the rss icon
  • - Some websites don't offer rss feeds, but there are other solutions, book a call with us to find out.
