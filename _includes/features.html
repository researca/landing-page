Discover, Read, Add to your newsletter

Any article you read on Researca can be added to your newsletter, we will use this to train ou AI and place on top of your feed articles that you will probably like.

Any URL you find can be added to your newsletter

Sometimes you will find cool ressources outside of Researca. But no worries, you're covered. Any article you read on your browser can be added to Researca using the browser extension.

And finally, export your newsletter issue

You can export your newsletter in markdown a format handled by most newsletter services. You can also import it in notion for final adjustments.
